AMARI is a fictional small digital services company created for this Web Technology I final project. The website demonstrates how a business can present its services, introduce its values and invite potential customers to make contact.

Our approach is intentionally straightforward: listen carefully, agree on the goal, create a practical solution and keep improving it. We believe that a website should be easy to use, easy to understand and useful on every screen size.
